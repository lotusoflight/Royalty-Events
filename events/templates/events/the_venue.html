Frequently Asked Questions

How Far In Advance Should I Book?-
We recommend booking 6 months in advance for weekend weddings and peak season galas to secure your desired date. For weekday celebrations and intimate private events, 2 to 4 months in advance is usually sufficient.
What Is Included In The Venue Rental Package?+
You can view what our rental packages have to offer by navigating to the wedding page or the celebrations page. Tables, chairs, lighting, suites, security and cleaning included.
What Is The Maximum Guest Capacity At LuxeSpace HTX?+
LuxeSpace comfortably accommodates up to 350 guests for a seated banquet dinner with dance floor. Up to 500 guests can be accommodated for cocktail style events.
Can I Bring My Own Outside Caterers & Vendors?+
Yes, we allow licensed outside caterers. For specialty vendors like florists, designers, DJs, event planners, and decorators, we also provide access to our curated signature vendor list featuring Houston's top event professionals.
Is Setup And Cleanup Included In The Venue Rental?+
Yes. Our team handles complete table and chair arrangement according to your custom floor plan prior to your event, as well as breakdown and cleanup after your celebration concludes.
